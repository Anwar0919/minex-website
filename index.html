Participation

How MineX check-ins may work

The planned first version is a daily check-in rewards system. A member signs in, checks in once per day and sees their recorded demo points.

📱

1. Join

Create an account when the app is ready.

⏱️

2. Check in

Complete one daily check-in under published rules.

🧾

3. Track points

View your activity history and points balance.

Important: Daily check-ins are not Bitcoin-style proof-of-work mining and do not generate cryptocurrency by themselves. MineX has no live mining, token, exchange listing or guaranteed rewards at this stage.
